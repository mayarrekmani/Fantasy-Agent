Who should you start?

Enter your Sleeper username. You get a recommended lineup for every league you are in, with the reasoning for each player, waiver pickups and trade ideas.

Optional: turn on Claude's final call

Paste your own Anthropic API key and Claude will check the latest news and make the final start/sit decision for each league. Your key is sent only with your request, is never stored, and you pay for your own usage (usually a few cents per league). Leave it blank for the projection model's picks.

Running this site yourself? Enter your owner passcode instead and the site uses its own key. It is remembered on this device only.

Projections start from Sleeper's own numbers and are adjusted slightly by a model that looks at current form, team performance, depth chart and matchups. They are estimates, not guarantees. Always check inactives before kickoff.

Not affiliated with Sleeper or the NFL. Player photos and team logos load from Sleeper's servers. Your username is only used to look up your public leagues.
